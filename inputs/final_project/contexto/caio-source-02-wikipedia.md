## Definition (consensus view)
The CAIO (chief artificial intelligence officer) is an executive who handles the implementation and/or development of AI within an organization. The essence of the role is **bridging business and AI** to achieve a desired outcome (IESE framing).

## Where the CAIO applies AI
- **Operational strategy** and **product differentiation**, working alongside other executives (Dice).
- New AI policies must align with digital transformation and business goals to improve operational efficiency, customer experience, or create new revenue (IBM).

## Practitioner perspective
- **Joe Mayberry (Head of AI, SailPoint), via Dice:** the CAIO makes corporate strategy succeed by **adapting the strategy itself** to reflect the company's market — i.e., AI reshapes strategy rather than just executing it.

## Expected competencies
- Fluency in AI technologies and practical understanding of how to work with AI.
- Technical skills: machine learning, data science and analytics, software development, AI infrastructure.
- Classic executive skills: management, **cultural leadership**, and an **ethical mindset**.

## Key takeaways for this project
- Confirms the **business–AI bridge** as the defining idea of the role.
- Adds **cultural leadership** (change management) as an explicit expectation — a criterion often missing from purely technical projects.
- Adds the notion that AI should **reshape strategy**, not only optimize existing processes: a useful test for whether a final project is transformational or merely incremental.
