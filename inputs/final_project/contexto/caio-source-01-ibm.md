## Core definition
The CAIO is a C-suite executive accountable for the **development, strategy and implementation of AI** across the organization. Having one signals a strong strategic commitment to AI and usually means substantial AI initiatives are already underway. The role offers a single point of **accountability and oversight** for the organization's engagement with AI.

## Why the role is emerging
- Generative AI (post-ChatGPT) made AI's value visible to every industry, not just early adopters.
- Implementations now involve many stakeholders and are complex, which calls for an owner.
- **Risk is as much a driver as opportunity:** large AI projects raise ethical questions and must navigate a shifting global regulatory landscape.
- **Statistic:** LinkedIn data (April 2024) shows the number of CAIOs nearly tripled over five years.

## Five areas of responsibility
| Area | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Strategic leadership** | Builds an AI strategy aligned with the digital-transformation roadmap and business goals; finds value in efficiency, customer experience and new revenue; secures executive buy-in and embeds AI into existing processes. |
| **Technology oversight** | Directs development and deployment of ML models and AI solutions; selects tools and methods; focuses effort on the highest-value use cases. |
| **Team management** | Builds and leads AI teams (data scientists, ML engineers, specialists); attracts talent; manages vendor partnerships. |
| **Ethics, governance & compliance** | Sets policies and frameworks for responsible AI; AI governance; addresses risk and bias; protects privacy and security; ensures regulatory compliance. |
| **Advocacy & education** | Educates internal and external stakeholders on the company's AI vision; acts as public spokesperson (podcasts, panels); positions the company as an attractive employer for AI talent. |

## Required skill profile
1. **Technical:** AI/ML, data science and analytics, traditional software development, AI infrastructure.
2. **Executive / business:** strategic vision, business acumen, storytelling tied to business goals, spotting AI-enabled market opportunities, running large cross-functional programs, winning funding and stakeholder enthusiasm.
3. **Ethical:** a strong ethical foundation so projects advance safely, securely and responsibly.

## Organizational placement
- The role is new and **not well-defined**; it overlaps with the CDO, CIO, CTO and CISO.
- **Reporting lines vary:** CEO, COO or CTO depending on existing structure.

## When an organization needs a CAIO
- AI is **central to products or services**.
- AI systems are **complex or span many stakeholders** and need integration and advocacy.
- A **strong data foundation already exists** (typically built by the CDO/CIO) that a CAIO can build on.
